# **PREDYS**
Dataset description ===  This is the dataset that accompanies the following publication in Preparation  
Title : "Neural Networks for Semantic and Syntactic Prediction and Visuo-Motor Statistical Learning in Adult Readers with and without Dyslexia."
Authors : Elisa Gavard, Valérie Chanoine, Franziska Geringswald, Jean-Luc Anton, Eddy Cavalli & Johannes C. Ziegler 
accepted in Neurobiology of Language

The dataset contains anatomical and functional MRI images
 of 49 partipants (25 typical and  24 dyslexic readers)

PREDYS

    	sub-{id}/fmap   --- functional
